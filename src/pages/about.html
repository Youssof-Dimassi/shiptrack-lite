About us

Shipping should never feel like guesswork

ShipTrack Lite helps people follow their shipments: where they are, where they've been, and when they move.

What we do

Every shipment gets a tracking number. With it, anyone can see the shipment's current status and every stop it has made along the way. Our team posts an update at each step, so the history is always up to date.

Customers who create a free account can save the shipments they care about and get an email each time one is updated, so they don't have to keep checking.

What we believe

  • Clear over clever. A status you can understand at a glance, in plain words.
  • Your inbox, your choice. We only email confirmed addresses, only about shipments you saved, and you can stop any time.
  • Privacy by default. We collect only what we need to run the service. Read how in our privacy policy.
