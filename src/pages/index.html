FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do I find my tracking number?

It's on the receipt or confirmation you got when the shipment was sent. It starts with ST, followed by 8 digits, for example ST10000001.

Do I need an account to track a shipment?

No. Anyone with the tracking number can see where it is. An account lets you save shipments and get an email when they're updated.

How do update emails work?

Save a shipment to your account and confirm your email address. Each time our team posts an update on that shipment, we email you the new status.

How do I stop the emails?

Every update email has a link to stop them, or you can turn them off on your My shipments page. You can turn them back on there too.

Who can see my shipments?

Your saved list is private to your account. A shipment's own status can be seen by anyone who has its tracking number, so share it only with people you trust.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot your password?" on the log-in page. We'll email you a link to choose a new one. The link works once and expires after an hour.
