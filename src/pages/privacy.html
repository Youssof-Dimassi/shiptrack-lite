Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information ShipTrack Lite collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and whether you've confirmed it. Your password is never stored, only a one-way scrambled version (a "hash") that can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your saved shipments: the shipments you choose to save, and whether you want update emails about them.
  • Messages you send us: your name, email, and message when you use the contact form.
  • Security information: to stop people guessing passwords or tracking numbers, we briefly count attempts from each internet address. These counts are stored in a scrambled form and expire within an hour.

Anyone can track a shipment without an account. We don't record which tracking numbers you look up.

Cookies and storage

  • One login cookie, set only when you log in, so the site knows it's you. It lasts up to 7 days, and logging out ends it.
  • Your light or dark mode choice, kept in your own browser only.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use analytics services.

Emails we send

  • Account emails you need: confirming your address and resetting your password.
  • Shipment update emails, only for shipments you saved, only to a confirmed address, and only while you have them turned on. Every one has a link to stop them.

We never send marketing emails.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's only handled by the services that run the website:

  • our email delivery provider, to send the emails above;
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the website's font. Your browser contacts Google's servers to load it, which shares your internet address with Google.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and saved shipments: until you delete your account.
  • Password reset links: one hour. Email confirmation links: 24 hours.
  • The contents of emails waiting to be sent are erased as soon as they're sent.

Your choices

  • Turn update emails on or off on your My shipments page.
  • Remove shipments from your saved list at any time.
  • Change your name, email address, or password, or delete your account, in Account settings.
  • To get a copy of your information, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
